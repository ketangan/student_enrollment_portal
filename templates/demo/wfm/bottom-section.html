Band Ensemble

Our flagship performance ensemble. Students train together, perform together, and represent WFM at events across Los Angeles and beyond.

Open Enrollment — All Experience Levels Welcome

What You'll Learn

  • Scales, technique, and tone development on your instrument
  • Sight-reading and music theory fundamentals
  • Ensemble playing and performance readiness
  • Leadership, discipline, and time management

Where You'll Perform

  • Community parades and civic events
  • Sports arenas and football stadiums
  • Concerts and entertainment shows
  • Scholarship showcases for college-bound students

Schedule Format

Weekly rehearsals plus performance dates throughout the year. Scheduling is confirmed after enrollment based on your availability. Evening and weekend slots available.

What to Bring

Your instrument if you have one — we also offer access to instruments through our rental program. No instrument? Our team will help you get set up before your first rehearsal.

Beginner-friendly: Beginner spots are available and open to students with no prior music experience. Our instructors meet every student at their level and build from there. Advanced and transfer students are equally welcome.
