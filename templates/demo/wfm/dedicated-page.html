How Enrollment Works

  • 1

    Submit your enrollment

    Complete our enrollment form in about 5 minutes. Tell us your student's age, instrument interest, and prior experience — this helps us place them in the right program from day one.

    Enroll Now → Opens the full enrollment form on its own page
  • 2

    We follow up within 1–2 business days

    Our admissions team reviews every enrollment and reaches out personally to confirm program availability, scheduling, and whether an audition or placement session is needed.

  • 3

    Audition or placement session (for ensemble entry)

    For Band and advanced instrument tracks, a brief placement session ensures your student is grouped with peers at the right level. Beginners are welcome — no experience required for beginner tracks.

  • 4

    Join the WFM family and start performing

    Once placed, your student joins weekly rehearsals and starts working toward performances at community events, parades, and venues across the region.
