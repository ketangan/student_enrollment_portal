Student Enrollment

Join one of LA's premier ensemble music programs. Students of all ages and experience levels are welcome.

WFM offers training in wind instruments, drums, piano, voice, music theory, and ensemble performance. Whether your student is picking up an instrument for the first time or looking to join a competitive performance group, we have a program for them.

Ready to Join the WFM Family?

Submit your enrollment form and our team will follow up within 1–2 business days to confirm scheduling, program placement, and next steps.

What happens after you submit

  • Within 1–2 business days: Our admissions team reviews your enrollment and reaches out to confirm program availability and scheduling.

  • Placement (for ensemble programs): We'll arrange a brief audition or placement session to ensure the right fit. Beginners skip this step.

  • Instrument access: Don't have an instrument? We'll connect you with our rental program before your first rehearsal.

  • Welcome to the ensemble: Your student joins weekly rehearsals and begins working toward performances at stadiums, parades, and community stages.
