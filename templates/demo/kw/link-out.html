Play For A Purpose

Our Programs

Five classrooms. One neighborhood. A community built around how children actually grow.

Kid Works offers programs for children ages 2 through 5, designed around play-based learning and social-emotional development. Each classroom is a world of its own — with experienced teachers, small group sizes, and a curriculum shaped by what each child is curious about.

Alongside our core classrooms, we offer Mindfulness & Yoga (weekly) and Growing in the Garden — a hands-on nature program that gives children a relationship with growing things, soil, and the outdoors.

We've been part of the Long Beach community for over 17 years. Enrollment is rolling, and spaces go quickly — particularly in the Bungalow (age 2) and Sun Room (ages 4–5).
