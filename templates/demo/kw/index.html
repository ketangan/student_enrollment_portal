How the enrollment form can
fit into your website

Below are four ways the Kid Works enrollment form can be added to your existing website. Each option uses the same form and the same admissions workflow — the only difference is how families access it.

Most seamless family experience: fully embedded on your Programs page.
Fastest to launch: link-out or dedicated page.

What stays the same

Your programs, branding, enrollment questions, child records, and follow-up workflow all stay exactly the same. These options only change how the form is presented to families on your website.

What this demo is showing

Each preview below shows the same Kid Works enrollment form placed into your site in a different way, so you can compare what feels most natural for families and easiest for your team to manage.

Option 1

Dedicated enrollment page

Clean and distraction-free

Families click "Enroll" and go straight to a dedicated full-page enrollment form. Simple, focused, and easy to share directly from social media or email.

  • Best when you want the form on its own standalone page
  • Simple to set up and maintain
  • Works great for mobile and direct social media links
View demo → Best for: simplicity
Option 2

Pop-up form on your existing page

Minimal change to your current site

Your current Programs page stays mostly as-is. Families click "Enroll Now" and the form opens in a modal overlay on top of the page.

  • Keeps your current page content intact
  • Feels lighter than sending families to a separate page
  • Good if you want minimal changes to your existing site
View demo → Best for: minimal disruption
Recommended

Fully embedded into the programs page

Most seamless experience for families

The enrollment form appears directly within your Programs page, below your program descriptions. Families stay on one page and the form feels like a natural part of your site.

  • Feels the most native to the Kid Works website
  • Best overall experience for parents
  • Strongest option for reducing drop-off
View demo → Best for: parent experience
Option 4

Open the form in a new tab

Fastest technical implementation

Your enrollment page includes a button that opens the full form in a new browser tab. The fastest and lowest-effort option to get started with zero changes to your layout.

  • Very easy to launch quickly
  • No embedded layout work needed
  • Works well on desktop and mobile
View demo → Best for: fastest rollout
Bonus

Interest / Inquiry Form

Capture families who aren't ready to enroll yet

A lightweight inquiry form — name, email, program interest — for families exploring options before they're ready to submit a full enrollment application. Responses appear in your admin leads pipeline instantly for follow-up.

  • Much shorter than the full enrollment form
  • Embeddable on any Contact or Programs page
  • Feeds directly into the admissions follow-up pipeline
View inquiry form → Embed preview → Best for: top-of-funnel

Recommendation for Kid Works Children's Center

Based on the current site structure, the fully embedded option is the strongest fit. It keeps families on your Programs page, preserves your existing content about each classroom and age group, and gives the enrollment form a permanent home without requiring a separate application site. For families discovering Kid Works through social media or neighbors, the dedicated page is the easiest link to share directly.
