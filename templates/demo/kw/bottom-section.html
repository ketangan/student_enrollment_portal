Our Programs

Sun Room — Ages 4–5

Home › Our Programs › Sun Room

The Sun Room is where children age 4 to 5 dive deeper into collaborative learning. They take on bigger projects, build planning and negotiation skills, and begin serving as mentors to younger children throughout the center. It's play with real purpose — the kind that builds the foundation for kindergarten readiness and lifelong curiosity.

What Children Explore

  • Collaborative, interest-based projects
  • Critical thinking and problem-solving through play
  • Language development and pre-literacy skills
  • Math concepts through building and games

Social-Emotional Growth

  • Planning, negotiation, and teamwork
  • Peer mentoring with younger classrooms
  • Conflict resolution with increasing independence
  • Self-regulation and emotional awareness

Schedule & Environment

  • Monday–Friday, 8am–5pm
  • Outdoor classroom time in the garden each day
  • Weekly Mindfulness & Yoga sessions
  • Small group projects with teacher guidance

Transition to Kindergarten

  • Kindergarten readiness curriculum built in
  • Portfolio documentation shared with families
  • Year-end family conferences
  • Referral letters and school placement support
