How Enrollment Works

  1. 1

    Submit this form

    Fill in your child's information, the program you're interested in, and any health or scheduling notes. Takes about 5 minutes.

    Start Enrollment → Opens the enrollment form
  2. 2

    We'll reach out within 1–2 business days

    Our team reviews each application and contacts you to discuss your child's age, program availability, and any special accommodations needed.

  3. 3

    Schedule a center tour (optional)

    We love having families come in and meet the teachers and see the classrooms. Tours are always welcome before finalizing enrollment.

  4. 4

    Complete enrollment paperwork

    Once confirmed, we'll send enrollment paperwork covering tuition, emergency contacts, health forms, and the family handbook.
