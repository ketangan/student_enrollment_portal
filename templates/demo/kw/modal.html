Play For A Purpose

Our Programs

Every classroom at Kid Works is designed around how children actually learn — through curiosity, play, and connection.

We offer five programs for children ages 2 through 5, plus supplemental experiences in Mindfulness & Yoga and Growing in the Garden. Each classroom builds on the last — routines and skills introduced at age 2 deepen and expand as children move through the center.

Our classrooms are intentionally small, with experienced teachers who know each child by name. Whether your child is just starting their first school experience or is getting ready for kindergarten, there's a place for them at Kid Works.

Programs fill on a rolling basis throughout the year. We encourage families to apply early — especially for the Bungalow (age 2) and Sun Room (ages 4–5), which are typically our most requested classrooms.
